# CP1 landing aircraft validation

Runtime source: `CP1_2024.glb`, unchanged (36,105,032 bytes). SolidWorks glTF 2.0 export; 106 nodes, 94 meshes, 805,260 triangles, no required extensions. Assembly bounds approximately 4.943 × 1.017 × 4.559 metres. Nose points along world -X; +Y is up.

The actual exported component name is `Propellor 28in-1` (node 105, mesh 93), a sibling of `CP1_Assembly_NoProp.STEP` under `CP1_2024`. Three.js sanitizes its display name; selection uses the preserved original `userData.name`. Local +Y maps to assembly -X. Its origin is on the shaft; no pivot correction or component repositioning is applied. The motor and propeller centerlines differ by only 2.046e-7 m in the exported transforms.

Supporting sources inspected: the SolidWorks assembly binary/header, both STEP file headers and component structure, and the propeller STEP cylindrical surfaces. STEP hub cylinders have x=z=0 and axes parallel to local Y, confirming the GLB origin/axis. Native SolidWorks was not available to resolve its proprietary mates; the assembled GLB motor and propeller transforms were numerically verified instead.

Only the propeller quaternion changes for spin: rest quaternion multiplied by rotation around local Y at +8 rad/s (counterclockwise viewed from the front, approximately 76 rpm for readable blades). A common outer group centers/scales the complete assembly and applies restrained glide. CAD structure is rendered as supplied; visible trusses are real model geometry, not an added wireframe.

Tune `AIRCRAFT_CONFIG` in `js/experience-config.js`: `propeller.radiansPerSecond`, `position`, `rotation`, `scale`, `camera.position`, `camera.target`, and `framing`. Terrain direction is +X, opposite the nose. Reduced motion freezes both propeller and glide.

Validation: `scripts/validate-aircraft.cjs` checks node selection, motor alignment, corrected counterclockwise rotation and unchanged body transforms. Browser regression scripts cover responsive layouts, scene disposal, intro, reduced motion, deep links, fallback and static subdirectory hosting. `scripts/render-aircraft-fallback.cjs` regenerates the static image. Official Three.js r180 GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils are vendored alongside the existing r180 runtime with relative imports.

## Hero interaction and stable contours (2026-09-23 refinement)

The dotted-circle module was removed, along with every scene import/update and the circles in the regenerated fallback. Editorial guides and text remain. Framing centerY is now -0.01, raised in two modest passes from -0.065. Matte surface color is now 0x414850; key/fill intensities are 2.8/1.55. Roughness remains 0.96 and metalness remains zero.

`js/aircraft-controls.js` provides pointer capture, a generous aircraft-envelope hit test, bounded yaw and view-relative tilt, cancellation, and arrow-key/Escape support. No pan, zoom, or free roll controls exist. Limits are +/-0.35 radians yaw (20 degrees) and +/-0.14 radians tilt (8 degrees). After 2500 ms without input, a 1200 ms smoothstep return removes the user rotation and frozen glide offset. The glide has its own clock, so freezing it does not stop the corrected propeller spin or terrain travel. Dismissal settles the model to its designed pose over 260 ms before its WebGL fade begins. Reduced motion keeps ambient animation off, follows user input directly, and returns without animation. The subtle hint disappears after the first interaction/dismissal and stays hidden for the session.

`js/contour-terrain.js` samples periodic terrain heights once into immutable geometry. Shared tiles translate rigidly; both surface and contour scalar move together. Vertex interpolation supplies the exact triangle height to the fragment shader, avoiding time-varying noise resampling. Analytic periodic-line coverage integrates over an fwidth pixel footprint, with CSS-pixel line width and a gradual distant-line fade. Tile boundaries match; world-space edge fading hides the outer extent. There are no per-frame vertex uploads or procedural threshold changes.

Tune `AIRCRAFT_CONFIG.framing.centerY` for vertical composition (larger moves up), `position` for world placement, `surface.color` and `lighting` for brightness, and `interaction.maxYaw/maxTilt` (radians), `sensitivity`, `idleTimeout`, `returnDuration`, `dismissReturnDuration` (milliseconds) for controls. `TERRAIN_CONFIG` exposes `lineWidth` (CSS pixels), `contourDensity` (levels per height unit), `contourOpacity`, `aliasFade` (pixel-footprint range), `forwardSpeed`, `cycles` (integer), `warp`, and `segments/lowSegments`.

Additional checks: `scripts/test-aircraft-interaction.cjs` observes actual renderer state through a test-only response injection to verify clamps, paused glide, continuing terrain/propeller, fixed camera, timeout return, cancellation, dismissal, keyboard and reduced-motion behavior. `scripts/test-terrain-stability.cjs` checks immutable vertex arrays, matching boundaries and image differences under a tracking camera and across a tile wrap at both quality settings.

## Mountain relief, weighted input, and editorial shell

Terrain heightScale is now 14 scene units with base elevation -8.2 and valleyScale 0.22. The fixed periodic depth envelope places the larger ridges behind the flight corridor and lower rolls in front. Measured vertex relief is approximately 10.7 units; background mean height is over twice foreground mean height. Vertex normals add restrained terrain-only relief lighting. Contour generation, analytic pixel coverage, immutable geometry, periodic movement and aft travel direction remain unchanged. The AA pixel tests continue to pass at both mesh resolutions, including the tile wrap.

Drag input drives a damped spring rather than directly assigning orientation. The controls integrate acceleration and velocity in substeps, clamp both orientation and angular speed, and project a small amount of momentum after release. `stiffness: 95`, `damping: 18`, `maxVelocity: 1.4` radians/second, `releaseMomentum: 0.07` seconds, and `deadzone: 1.5` CSS pixels tune the response. Existing yaw/tilt limits remain unchanged. Return starts after 2500 ms without interaction, takes 1200 ms, and preserves the existing 260 ms dismissal settle. The base Z rotation is -3 degrees because the source nose points along -X; a transformed nose-vector check verifies exactly 3 degrees upward in the reduced-motion/default pose.

The sidebar markup is in index.html and its layout is in css/landing.css. `--rail-padding-x`, `--rail-padding-y` and `--rail-gap` control spacing; compact-desktop and mobile overrides preserve access to all content. The Home-only About/Footer visibility rule uses data-view=landing, so Portfolio deep links and the Resume footer remain intact. Home, Portfolio, Resume and the Resources index share .site-header/.brand/.primary-nav from styles.css, with --header-height and --header-padding controlling geometry at the existing breakpoints. Resources' standalone PDF document toolbar is preserved.
